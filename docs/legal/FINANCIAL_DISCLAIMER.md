# MSD's Financial Assistant

## Financial Disclaimer

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 23 August 2026

## Important

MSD's Financial Assistant is a **software assistant for personal financial management**.

It is designed to help users organize financial information, record financial activity, perform calculations and better understand information they provide.

It is not a substitute for professional financial or other qualified advice.

## 1. Not Professional Advice

Information provided by the Application should not be treated as:

* Financial advice
* Investment advice
* Tax advice
* Legal advice
* Accounting advice
* Banking advice
* Insurance advice
* Credit advice
* Any other professional advice

Where professional advice is appropriate, consult an appropriately qualified professional.

## 2. Your Financial Decisions

You are responsible for your own financial decisions.

Before making an important decision involving:

* Investments
* Loans
* Debt repayment
* Taxes
* Insurance
* Retirement
* Major purchases
* Financial commitments

you should independently verify the relevant information and consider obtaining appropriate professional advice.

## 3. Calculations

The Application may calculate or display:

* Account balances
* Income
* Expenses
* Savings
* Savings rates
* Loan payments
* Interest
* Financial projections
* Comparisons
* Other financial figures

Calculations depend on the information entered by the user and the assumptions used by the Application.

Incorrect, incomplete or outdated information may produce incorrect results.

Important calculations should be independently verified before being relied upon.

## 4. AI-Generated Information

Future versions of the Application may provide AI-assisted financial insights.

AI-generated responses may contain:

* Errors
* Incorrect assumptions
* Missing information
* Outdated information
* Misinterpretations

AI-generated information is intended as informational assistance and should not be treated as professional advice.

**Do not make a significant financial decision solely because an AI response suggested it.**

Important information should be independently verified.

## 5. No Guaranteed Results

The Application does not guarantee:

* Savings
* Investment returns
* Debt reduction
* Interest savings
* Financial improvement
* Investment performance
* Loan outcomes
* Any other particular financial result

Actual financial outcomes depend on circumstances outside the control of the Application.

## 6. User-Provided Information

The Application relies substantially on information entered by the user.

Users are responsible for reviewing:

* Account balances
* Transaction information
* Interest rates
* Loan information
* Dates
* Amounts
* Other financial inputs

## 7. No Banking Credentials Required

The Application does not require:

* OTPs
* UPI PINs
* ATM PINs
* Debit-card PINs
* Credit-card PINs
* CVVs
* Internet-banking passwords
* Bank login passwords

**Never provide these credentials to anyone claiming to represent MSD's Financial Assistant.**

## 8. Final Responsibility

MSD's Financial Assistant is intended to **assist you, not make financial decisions for you**.

Use the information provided by the Application as one input into your decision-making process.

Apply your own judgment, independently verify important information and obtain professional advice where appropriate.
