# MSD's Financial Assistant

## Privacy Notice

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 23 August 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Notice explains how MSD's Financial Assistant processes information when you create an account and use the Application.

MSD's Financial Assistant is a personal financial-management software application.

This Notice describes the information currently processed by the Application, the purposes for which it is used, the services enabled by that processing, and important information about your choices and responsibilities.

## 2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on how you use the Application.

### 2.1 Registration and Account Information

When you register, the Application may process:

* Full name
* Email address
* Authentication information associated with your account

Firebase Authentication is currently used to provide account authentication.

### 2.2 Financial Account Information

When you use the Accounts feature, you may provide:

* Account name
* Account type
* Opening balance
* Currency
* Active/inactive account status
* Account creation information
* Account update information

For example, you may create an account representing a bank account, cash account, credit card or other financial account.

### 2.3 Future Financial Information

As additional features are introduced, the Application may allow you to voluntarily provide information such as:

* Income
* Expenses
* Transfers
* Budgets
* Loans
* Investments
* Financial goals
* Financial notes

Only information associated with features that you actually use will be processed.

The Application should not claim to collect or process future categories of information before those features are implemented.

## 3. Information We Do Not Require

**MSD's Financial Assistant does not require your confidential banking credentials.**

You should never provide the Application with:

* OTPs
* UPI PINs
* ATM PINs
* Debit-card PINs
* Credit-card PINs
* CVVs
* Internet-banking passwords
* Bank login passwords

These credentials are not required for the Application's personal financial-management functionality.

## 4. Important Security Warning

Never enter confidential banking credentials into:

* Account names
* Notes
* Transaction descriptions
* AI conversations
* Support messages
* Any other Application field

Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV, password or banking credential with anyone claiming to represent MSD's Financial Assistant.

## 5. Why We Process Information

The Application processes information for purposes connected with providing and securing the Service.

### 5.1 Account Creation and Authentication

Information is processed to:

* Create your Application account
* Authenticate you
* Maintain your authenticated session
* Allow you to access your account

### 5.2 Financial Management

Information you voluntarily provide is processed to:

* Create and manage your financial accounts
* Store your financial information
* Display your accounts
* Calculate financial summaries
* Provide features requested by you

### 5.3 Security

Information may be processed to:

* Authenticate users
* Enforce access controls
* Prevent unauthorized access
* Protect users and the Application
* Maintain the security of the Service

### 5.4 Service Operation and Maintenance

Information and technical data may be processed where necessary to:

* Operate the Application
* Maintain reliability
* Diagnose technical problems
* Maintain and improve the Service

The Application will not claim to collect information for advertising, profiling, analytics or other purposes unless such functionality is actually implemented.

## 6. How Financial Information Is Used

Financial information that you enter is used to provide the financial-management features you request.

For example, an account balance may be used to calculate and display financial summaries.

The Application does not require you to provide banking credentials in order to use its financial-management functionality.

## 7. Firebase and Third-Party Services

The Application currently uses Firebase services.

Firebase Authentication is used for user authentication.

Cloud Firestore is used to store application data associated with authenticated users.

The Application is designed around user-owned data, with authenticated access controls intended to prevent one user from accessing another user's financial data.

Third-party services may have their own terms, privacy practices, technical requirements and service limitations.

The Application does not claim that third-party services are controlled by the Application.

## 8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the Application.

The Application uses authenticated access and database security rules intended to restrict users to their own financial information.

However, no internet-connected service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are also responsible for:

* Protecting your password
* Securing your device
* Not sharing your account credentials
* Avoiding entry of confidential banking credentials

## 9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

* Provide the Service
* Maintain your account
* Provide features you request
* Maintain security
* Comply with applicable legal requirements
* Resolve disputes
* Protect the Service and its users

Specific retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the relevant feature.

The Application will not claim a specific retention period unless that period has been established and implemented.

## 10. Deletion of Personal Data

Deleting the Application from your device does not necessarily delete information stored on the Application's servers or third-party infrastructure.

Where a self-service account or data-deletion mechanism is available, users may use that mechanism.

If such a mechanism is not yet available, users may submit a request through the official contact mechanism made available by the Application.

Requests may be subject to applicable law, security requirements, legitimate retention requirements and technical considerations.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal data.

These may include rights concerning:

* Access to personal data
* Correction of inaccurate information
* Deletion where applicable
* Withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis of processing
* Other rights available under applicable law

Requests should be submitted through the official contact mechanism made available by the Application.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 establishes rights and obligations concerning personal-data processing, including notice, consent and certain user rights, subject to its applicability and commencement.

## 12. Consent and Notice

Where consent is required and used as the basis for processing, the Application will seek consent through a clear and understandable mechanism.

The Privacy Notice is intended to explain:

* What personal information is processed
* Why it is processed
* What services or features the processing enables

The Application will not intentionally use vague or misleading consent language.

The Privacy Notice is separate from the Terms of Service.

## 13. Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing is based on consent and applicable law provides a right to withdraw that consent, users may use the mechanism provided by the Application or the official contact mechanism.

Withdrawal should be reasonably easy to request.

Withdrawal of consent may affect the availability of features that depend on the relevant processing.

Withdrawal does not necessarily invalidate processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing based on another lawful basis where applicable.

## 14. Data Sharing

The Application may use third-party service providers that are necessary to operate the Service.

The current Application uses Firebase services for authentication and application data storage.

The Application does not intentionally sell users' personal financial information.

The Application should not claim that personal information is shared for advertising or behavioral profiling unless such functionality is actually implemented and appropriately disclosed.

## 15. Advertising and Tracking

The current Application does not require users to provide financial information for advertising purposes.

No claim is made here regarding analytics, advertising or tracking technologies beyond functionality actually implemented in the Application.

If such technologies are introduced in the future, this Notice will be updated as appropriate.

## 16. AI and Financial Information

Future versions of the Application may introduce AI-assisted financial features.

Before introducing functionality that sends personal or financial information to an external AI service, the relevant privacy documentation should be updated to explain:

* What information is sent
* Why it is sent
* Which service processes it
* How it is handled
* Any relevant retention or usage practices

The current Application should not claim that users' financial information is being sent to an external AI provider unless such functionality has actually been implemented.

## 17. Children's Data

The Application is intended for users who are legally capable of using the Service.

The Application does not require confidential banking credentials from children or other users.

Where applicable law imposes additional requirements concerning children or minors, those requirements will be followed.

## 18. Changes to This Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice may be updated when:

* New features are introduced
* Data-processing practices change
* Legal requirements change
* Security practices change
* The Application's infrastructure changes

The current version and effective date should be made available through the Application.

Where required by applicable law, material changes will be communicated through an appropriate mechanism.

Privacy-related questions, complaints or requests should be submitted through the official contact mechanism made available by MSD's Financial Assistant.
